# Problem Statement

Modern power grids are facing unprecedented challenges due to aging infrastructure, extreme weather events, and increasing demand. Currently, grid operators rely heavily on reactive maintenance and siloed data systems. 

This traditional approach leads to several critical issues:
1. **Prolonged Outages:** Without predictive capabilities, operators can only react to failures after they occur, resulting in extended downtime for critical infrastructure and communities.
2. **High Operational Costs:** Emergency repairs and unplanned downtime are significantly more expensive than planned maintenance.
3. **Safety Risks:** Catastrophic failures, such as transformer explosions or thermal runaway, pose severe risks to utility workers and the public.
4. **Data Fragmentation:** Telemetry data, weather forecasts, and maintenance logs are often kept in separate systems, making it difficult to form a cohesive, real-time understanding of grid health.

Grid operators need a centralized, intelligent system that can predict failures, simulate stress scenarios, and provide actionable intelligence before a crisis occurs.
